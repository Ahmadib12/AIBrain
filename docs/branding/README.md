# AIBrain visual identity

## Primary logo

The primary scalable logo is [`assets/branding/aibrain-logo.svg`](../../assets/branding/aibrain-logo.svg).

The emblem combines two equal ideas in one sphere:

- **Left half — neural brain:** intelligence, learning, reasoning, and adaptation.
- **Right half — Earth:** global reach, communication, and assistance across borders.
- **Orbit — connection:** the assistant connects intelligence with the user's world.
- **Blue palette — technology and trust:** use the existing cyan/blue gradient on dark navy backgrounds.

## Brand line

**Your World. Your Intelligence. Your Control.**

## Usage

- Prefer the SVG source for websites, documentation, and scalable interfaces.
- Keep the full emblem and wordmark proportions intact; do not stretch or recolor individual halves.
- Use a dark navy background for the primary presentation.
- For small app icons, use the brain/globe emblem alone and omit the wordmark and tagline. Export platform-specific PNG/ICO assets from the SVG when the Android and desktop client projects are introduced.
- Provide sufficient clear space around the mark and do not place text or controls over the emblem.

## Implementation status

This commit adds the brand source asset and its usage guidance to the repository. It does not claim that an Android app, desktop app, website, or application launcher currently exists or has been rebranded; those client surfaces should consume this shared asset when their UI projects are added.
