# AIBrain visual identity

## Official master artwork

The official AIBrain logo is the exact user-approved artwork showing a luminous electric-blue neural brain on the left and a realistic Earth globe on the right, joined into one sphere, with a glowing orbital ring, the **AIBrain** wordmark, and the tagline:

**YOUR WORLD. YOUR INTELLIGENCE. YOUR CONTROL.**

The uploaded 1536 × 1024 image is the visual source of truth. Do not redraw, simplify, recolor, rearrange, or substitute a different brain/globe illustration when applying the official logo. Preserve the original composition, proportions, lighting, typography, colors, and tagline.

## Repository asset status

- `assets/branding/aibrain-logo.svg` is an earlier editable concept only. It is **not** the approved master artwork and must not be presented as an exact copy.
- The approved uploaded raster artwork still needs to be committed as a binary image asset (for example, `assets/branding/aibrain-logo.png`). The current GitHub file-writing connection only accepts UTF-8 text for file creation and cannot transfer this uploaded PNG as binary bytes, so that exact binary asset has not yet been added.
- Once the master PNG is committed, update app icons and web/desktop/Android surfaces from that master without altering the artwork. Any icon-specific crops or transparent variants should be separately exported and approved.

## Brand line

**Your World. Your Intelligence. Your Control.**

## Usage

- Use the approved master artwork as supplied for the primary brand lockup.
- Do not stretch, crop, retype, or rebuild the logo.
- Keep the full tagline when there is enough space; use a separately approved emblem-only export for small app icons.
- Maintain strong contrast and adequate clear space around the artwork.
